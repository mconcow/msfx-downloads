## MSFX 1.0.0, the first release 🎉

**[⬇ Download MSFX-Setup.exe](https://github.com/mconcow/msfx-downloads/releases/latest/download/MSFX-Setup.exe)**. Run it, then open **Window > Extensions > MSFX** in Premiere Pro or After Effects.

### What's in it
- 902 sound effects in groups, with hover preview and favourites
- Effects rack, tube synth, three amp designs, live meters and a nixie clock
- Works in Premiere Pro and After Effects 2022 and newer on Windows
- Sign in with your email and a 6-digit code, with no password
- One-click updates and new sound packs inside the panel

### Files
| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| **MSFX-Setup.exe** | The installer. This is the only file you need. |
| MSFX-app.msfxrelease | The MSFX panels, signed. For offline installs only. |
| MSFX-library.msfxrelease | The sound library, signed. For offline installs only. |

**Offline install:** put all three files in one folder and run MSFX-Setup.exe.

> "Windows protected your PC"? Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
